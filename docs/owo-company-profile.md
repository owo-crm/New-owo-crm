The company is being built as a local digital growth and business-solutions agency for small and medium-sized businesses.

The agency combines marketing, content production, online visibility, paid acquisition, website development, and custom digital tools. Its purpose is not simply to sell isolated marketing tasks, but to help clients attract customers, improve their digital presence, and make selected business processes easier to manage.

The company intends to work locally where in-person collaboration creates value, while also delivering remote strategy, production, development, analytics, and ongoing support.

## 2. Geographic focus

The initial service area is **Trójmiasto**:

- Gdańsk
- Gdynia
- Sopot

Local presence is an important part of the intended model. Depending on the project, the team may visit client premises for discovery meetings, photography, video production, and content gathering.

Expansion to other areas of Poland may be considered after the local offer and delivery model have been validated.

## 3. Mission and intended value

**Mission:** Help local businesses grow and operate more effectively by combining practical marketing execution with appropriate digital solutions.

The agency aims to help clients:

1. Become easier to discover online.
2. Present their brand and offer professionally.
3. Generate relevant enquiries, bookings, visits, and sales.
4. Build a consistent and useful social-media presence.
5. Measure marketing activity and improve it over time.
6. Reduce manual work through websites, integrations, and tailored software where justified.

The agency should focus on business outcomes rather than vanity metrics. Reach, views, and follower counts matter only when they support a meaningful client objective.

## 4. Positioning

**Working positioning:** Local Growth & Digital Solutions Agency.

The company sits at the intersection of:

- Digital marketing and performance advertising.
- Social-media management and content production.
- Website design and development.
- Local SEO and online reputation.
- Business process digitisation and custom micro-software.

The intended differentiator is the ability to connect these services into a coherent solution. For example, a client may receive content production, social-media distribution, paid campaigns, a conversion-focused landing page, lead tracking, and a simple CRM workflow as parts of one growth system.

Do not claim that this combination is unique in the market without competitor research.

## 5. Target customers

Potential segments to validate include:

- Fitness studios and local activity providers.
- Retail shops and local product brands.
- Home, repair, and other service businesses.

These are hypotheses, not confirmed priority segments. Prioritisation should consider willingness to pay, customer lifetime value, sales accessibility, competition, delivery effort, and the potential for recurring work.

## 8. Principles and standards

The company should operate according to these principles:

1. **Results before activity.** Measure work by its contribution to the client's business objective, not by the number of posts or tasks completed.
2. **Honest expectations.** Do not promise guaranteed rankings, sales, leads, or advertising returns.
3. **Clear scope.** Specify deliverables, revision limits, response times, dependencies, and exclusions.
4. **Transparency.** Make reporting understandable and distinguish agency fees from media spend and third-party costs.
5. **Fit-for-purpose technology.** Recommend custom software only when it solves a real problem better than a suitable existing tool.
6. **Responsible data handling.** Treat client and customer data carefully and account for applicable GDPR/RODO obligations.
7. **Continuous improvement.** Review campaign performance, client feedback, delivery time, and profitability.
8. **Sustainable operations.** Avoid accepting work that the team cannot deliver to an appropriate standard.

## 10. Guidance for Claude

When assisting this company:

- Treat this file as the baseline company context, not as proof that every planned service is already operational.
- Distinguish between **currently available capabilities**, **planned services**, and **future possibilities**.
- Do not present an unvalidated idea as an established company policy.
- Prefer practical, low-overhead recommendations suitable for a new agency.
- Consider the Polish market and the realities of selling to local businesses in Trójmiasto.
- Use PLN for financial estimates and distinguish net (netto) from gross (brutto) amounts.
- When discussing Polish legal, tax, advertising, privacy, or consumer requirements, verify current rules rather than relying on assumptions.
- Prepare client-facing Polish materials in natural, professional Polish unless another language is requested.
- Challenge weak assumptions respectfully and explain trade-offs.
- Do not invent client results, testimonials, team credentials, equipment, or partnerships.
